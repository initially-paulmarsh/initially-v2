Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Initially ("we," "our," or "the app") is a daily puzzle game. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

Account information: When you sign in, we collect your email address via a secure sign-in link (magic link authentication). We do not collect or store passwords.

Gameplay data: We store your puzzle results, including which puzzles you've played, your guesses, win/loss outcomes, current streak, and guess statistics. This is used to sync your progress and stats across your devices.

We do not collect: location data, contacts, photos, or any device data beyond what's needed for the app to function.

How We Use Your Information

  • To let you sign in and keep your streak/stats synced across devices
  • To improve the app and understand how puzzles are played
  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not show third-party ads

Third-Party Services

We use the following services to run the app:

  • Supabase (database and authentication) — stores your account and gameplay data securely
  • Netlify (hosting) — serves the app itself

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

Initially is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
